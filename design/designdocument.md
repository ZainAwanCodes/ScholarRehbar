# ScholarRehbar – Landing Page Design Document

## 1. Project Overview

### Product Name
ScholarRehbar

### Tagline
“Uncovering official scholarships, eliminating fake promises.”

### Purpose
ScholarRehbar is a SaaS-based scholarship guidance platform designed to help young students discover official scholarships, avoid consultancy scams, and independently complete their study abroad journey with trusted guidance and support.

The landing page will serve as:
- A branding platform
- A trust-building interface
- A lead generation system
- A conversion-focused SaaS landing page
